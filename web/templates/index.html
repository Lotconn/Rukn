Munadi

This is the official site for the Munadi App, developed to work under any GNU/Linux, Windows, Android and Raspberry Pi. Currently, downloadable builds are available for GNU/Linux and Android only.

Downloads »

Features

  • Prayer times for almost any city.
  • Light and free from advertisements.
  • Toggle between Hijri and Georgian cals.
  • Ability to select your preferred Math'hab.
  • Ability to adjust prayer time +/- manually.
  • Ability to enable, mute or adjust Athan volume.
  • Choose from 10 different popular calculation methods.
