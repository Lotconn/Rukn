The Project

We strive to create beautiful Athan applications that are opensource, free-of-cost, free of advertisements, tracking or any other invasive things and compatible with diverse devices and operating systems.

The project is hosted at GitLab. Anybody willing to contribute to the project is welcomed. Please feel free to share your ideas, thoughts, bug-reports, feature requests, code contributions or discussions about where help is needed.
